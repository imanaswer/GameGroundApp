| 16 | 2 Aug 2026 | **The §3 perspective tilt is suspended app-wide — `tilt` is opt-in and nothing opts in.** Supersedes Decision 15's cards-only carve-out, one control later: the same tearing was reported switching Camps/Workshops/Events, because `SegmentedControl` also renders `Press` and still took the `tilt=true` default. | 15 | 2 Aug 2026 | **The §3 perspective tilt is cards-only; buttons compress on scale alone.** Reported on device: tapping a coach batch row split it into top and bottom halves that repainted separately. Cause was `Press` applying `{perspective: 600}` + `rotateX`, which promotes the view into a 3D compositing layer regardless of angle; on iOS those layers rasterize in tiles and tear. Two changes: `Press` now omits perspective entirely at rest (an untouched control was permanently 3D for a tilt that only exists mid-press), and `Button` passes `tilt={false}` so the tilt survives only where it is both visible and on a large static surface — cards. At button size a 2.4° rotateX is imperceptible; the felt feedback is the scale and the haptic. Precedent: Decision 11 dropped expo-blur from the tab bar for the same reason — a spec'd effect that does not survive contact with the device yields to the device. MOTION.md §3 revised. Reported by Anaswer; found only because the app finally ran on hardware. | Anaswer |
| 14 | 2 Aug 2026 | **Bottom sheets slide on a curve, not a spring.** `Sheet.tsx` opened with `spring.pop` (damping 14 / stiffness 220, ζ≈0.47) — a ~19% overshoot over a full screen-height travel, i.e. a visible bounce on the checkout sheet. First corrected to the spec-designated `spring.sheet` (ζ≈0.68, ~5%), still perceptibly springy on device. Resolved by moving entry/exit to `withTiming`: in on `dur.base` + `ease.exit`, out on `dur.fast`, drag snap-back on `dur.base`. Rationale: any underdamped spring overshoots by definition, and the sheet is the widest-travel element in the app, so the artifact is most visible exactly where it matters least — a payment sheet should feel deliberate, not playful. `spring.sheet` is now unused; retained in §1 for a future settle-physics consumer. Also removed the two inline durations (220/200ms) this replaced, restoring §1's "nothing inline, ever". Reported from device by Anaswer. | Anaswer |
